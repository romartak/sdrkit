# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in SDR-Kit a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation. English and Spanish are both welcome everywhere in this project.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind, patient and welcoming — many people arrive here building their first radio project
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community and taking responsibility for our mistakes

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, trolling, or personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Sharing intercepted private communications or content that identifies people who were overheard
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit, or reject
contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct. Report incidents privately to the maintainers
through [GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/romartak/sdrkit/security/advisories/new) or by
contacting the repository owner on GitHub. All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

Consequences follow the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.
